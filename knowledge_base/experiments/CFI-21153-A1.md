## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21153-A1 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-06-02 with 30% ethane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 38.87%, a pressure of
50.7 kPa and a fuel flow of 2.36 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and self-extinguished after 59.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
198.9 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 29.8 of 100, a **medium** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
