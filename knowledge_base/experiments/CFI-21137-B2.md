## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21137-B2 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-05-17 with 30% propane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 37.12%, a pressure of
101.3 kPa and a fuel flow of 1.5 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and was still burning when the fuel flow was cut after 147.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
161.4 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 77.0 of 100, a **high** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
