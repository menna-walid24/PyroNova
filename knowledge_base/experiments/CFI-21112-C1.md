## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21112-C1 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-04-22 with propane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 37.77%, a pressure of
94.4 kPa and a fuel flow of 0.5 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and was still burning when the fuel flow was cut after 29.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
529.3 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 59.4 of 100, a **high** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
