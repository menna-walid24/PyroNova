## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21162-A2 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-06-11 with 30% ethane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 39.5%, a pressure of
101.3 kPa and a fuel flow of 1.5 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and self-extinguished after 71.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
261.7 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 32.8 of 100, a **medium** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
