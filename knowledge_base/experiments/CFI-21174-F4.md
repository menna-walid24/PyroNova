## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21174-F4 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-06-23 with 30% butane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 38.41%, a pressure of
202.7 kPa and a fuel flow of 1.5 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and was still burning when the fuel flow was cut after 35.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
242.0 °C. Cool flame: no.

The hazard index is 60.2 of 100, a **high** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
