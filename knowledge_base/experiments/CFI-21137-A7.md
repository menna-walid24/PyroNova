## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21137-A7 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-05-17 with 30% propane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 35.24%, a pressure of
101.3 kPa and a fuel flow of 1.2 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and self-extinguished after 33.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
229.6 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 23.2 of 100, a **medium** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
