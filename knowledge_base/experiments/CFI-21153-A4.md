## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21153-A4 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-06-02 with 30% ethane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 33.49%, a pressure of
50.7 kPa and a fuel flow of 0.5 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and was still burning when the fuel flow was cut after 56.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
508.7 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 63.4 of 100, a **high** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
