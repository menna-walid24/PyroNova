## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21106-C1 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-04-16 with 50% butane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 37.72%, a pressure of
50.7 kPa and a fuel flow of 1.3 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and self-extinguished after 63.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
242.0 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 30.8 of 100, a **medium** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
