# Comparison of fuels — ethane, propane, butane

## Summary

By fuel:
- **ethane** (41 tests, O2 18.0-40.0%): 40 ignited, 30 self-extinguished, 10 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 49.0 s, longest 304.0 s; mean hazard index 37.5/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21162-A4, CFI-21153-C4, CFI-21153-A5.
- **propane** (56 tests, O2 20.9-40.0%): 40 ignited, 34 self-extinguished, 6 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 11.0 s, longest 259.0 s; mean hazard index 19.7/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21112-A6, CFI-21112-A4, CFI-21137-B2.
- **butane** (82 tests, O2 20.7-39.9%): 52 ignited, 38 self-extinguished, 14 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 40.0 s, longest 251.0 s; mean hazard index 25.0/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21174-C6, CFI-21291-C1, CFI-21174-A4.

By fuel mix (diluted mixes contain nitrogen):
- **30% butane** (52 tests, O2 21.1-39.9%): 34 ignited, 25 self-extinguished, 9 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 35.0 s, longest 251.0 s; mean hazard index 26.6/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21174-C6, CFI-21291-C1, CFI-21174-A4.
- **30% ethane** (27 tests, O2 20.7-40.0%): 26 ignited, 21 self-extinguished, 5 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 56.0 s, longest 304.0 s; mean hazard index 37.3/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21162-A4, CFI-21153-C4, CFI-21153-A5.
- **30% propane** (36 tests, O2 20.9-40.0%): 27 ignited, 25 self-extinguished, 2 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 6.0 s, longest 147.0 s; mean hazard index 17.3/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21137-B2, CFI-21137-B1, CFI-21137-A3.
- **50% butane** (30 tests, O2 20.7-39.6%): 18 ignited, 13 self-extinguished, 5 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 41.0 s, longest 120.0 s; mean hazard index 22.2/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21106-D1, CFI-21102-C3, CFI-21106-F1.
- **ethane** (14 tests, O2 18.0-39.4%): 14 ignited, 9 self-extinguished, 5 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 42.0 s, longest 127.0 s; mean hazard index 37.8/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21088-H2, CFI-21088-K1, CFI-21088-H1.
- **propane** (20 tests, O2 21.1-39.5%): 13 ignited, 9 self-extinguished, 4 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 29.0 s, longest 259.0 s; mean hazard index 24.1/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21112-A6, CFI-21112-A4, CFI-21112-A3.

Caution: each fuel was run on different days and at different oxygen/pressure conditions, so
this is not a controlled fuel-versus-fuel comparison.
