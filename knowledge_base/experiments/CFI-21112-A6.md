## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21112-A6 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-04-22 with propane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 37.65%, a pressure of
94.4 kPa and a fuel flow of 1.0 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and self-extinguished after 259.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
460.5 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 45.0 of 100, a **high** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
