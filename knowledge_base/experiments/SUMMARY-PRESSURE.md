# Effect of pressure — all tests

## Summary

- **51 kPa** (25 tests, O2 20.7-39.3%): 20 ignited, 16 self-extinguished, 4 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 60.0 s, longest 274.0 s; mean hazard index 31.7/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21153-A5, CFI-21153-A3, CFI-21174-A4.
- **94 kPa** (22 tests, O2 36.5-39.5%): 18 ignited, 9 self-extinguished, 9 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 44.0 s, longest 259.0 s; mean hazard index 38.8/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21112-A6, CFI-21112-A4, CFI-21088-H2.
- **101 kPa** (83 tests, O2 18.0-40.0%): 54 ignited, 47 self-extinguished, 7 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 22.0 s, longest 304.0 s; mean hazard index 19.1/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21162-A4, CFI-21162-A3, CFI-21291-G1.
- **203 kPa** (37 tests, O2 21.0-39.9%): 32 ignited, 22 self-extinguished, 10 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 35.0 s, longest 276.0 s; mean hazard index 36.1/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21153-C4, CFI-21174-C6, CFI-21291-C1.
- **304 kPa** (12 tests, O2 21.6-21.7%): 8 ignited, 8 self-extinguished, 0 still burning when the fuel flow was cut; median burn time 5.0 s, longest 6.0 s; mean hazard index 10.5/100. Longest-burning tests: CFI-21130-N1, CFI-21130-N2, CFI-21130-N3.

Pressure and oxygen were changed together across test days, so pressure effects are mixed with
oxygen and fuel effects.
