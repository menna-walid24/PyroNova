Experiment CFI-21291-A1 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-10-18 with 30% butane as fuel.

202.7 kPa and a fuel flow of 1.5 mg/s.

364.0 °C. Cool flame: yes.
