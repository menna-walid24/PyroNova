# Experiment CFI-21102-C1

## Investigation

- **Source Investigation:** PSI-159 (CFI-G) — NASA Cool Flames Investigation with Gases, normal (fuel-into-oxidizer) gaseous diffusion flames
- **Platform:** International Space Station (microgravity)
- **PSI-159 Test ID:** PSI159-21102C1
- **Test Date:** 2021-04-12

## Fuel

- **Fuel:** butane
- **Fuel Mix:** 50% butane
- **Fuel Mole Fraction (Xf):** 0.5
- **Fuel Bottle Concentration:** 50.0 %

## Experimental Conditions

- **Oxygen Concentration (before test):** 39.3 %
- **Oxygen Concentration (after test):** 39.05 %
- **Oxygen Band:** enriched (33-40% test-chamber condition)
- **Pressure:** 101.3 kPa (1.013 bar)
- **Fuel Flow:** 1.6 mg/s
- **Adiabatic Flame Temperature:** 2693.5 K

## Observed Results

- **Hot Ignition:** yes
- **Burn Time:** 41.0 s
- **End Mode:** self_extinguished
- **Peak Burner Temperature:** 269.0 °C
- **Cool Flame:** not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291)
- **Outcome:** self_extinguished
- **Data Quality Flags:** none
- **Crew Comments:** Extinction detected. Fuel flow stopped in 6 sec after PMT dropped to 0.

## Fire Risk

- **Hazard Index (Risk Score):** 25.2 of 100
- **Risk Level:** medium

## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21102-C1 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-04-12 with 50% butane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 39.3%, a pressure of
101.3 kPa and a fuel flow of 1.6 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and self-extinguished after 41.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
269.0 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 25.2 of 100, a **medium** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
