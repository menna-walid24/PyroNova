## Experimental Summary

Experiment CFI-21106-F1 is a real ISS test from PSI-159 (CFI-G)
run on 2021-04-16 with 50% butane as fuel.

The test used an oxygen concentration of 35.85%, a pressure of
50.7 kPa and a fuel flow of 0.8 mg/s.

Result: the hot flame ignited and was still burning when the fuel flow was cut after 72.0 s. Peak burner temperature was
505.0 °C. Cool flame: not examined (cool flames were only examined on test days 21174 and 21291).

The hazard index is 65.8 of 100, a **high** risk level.
Note: The 33-40% oxygen levels are ISS test-chamber conditions, not a spacecraft cabin atmosphere (ISS cabin air is about 21%).
